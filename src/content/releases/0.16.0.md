Two things happened since the last release. People started using Brainstorm, so I
went through it the way a new person does and fixed what got in the way. And the
features that did not make 0.15.0 are here.

## Ask the agent in Chat

Type `@agent` in any channel, followed by what you want. The agent answers in
the thread, and the reply is marked as asked by you.

When it wants to create something, it does not do it. It shows a card with the
task or note it proposes, and waits. Only the person who asked can approve.

<figure>
  <img src="/screenshots/0.16.0/chat-agent-proposal.webp" alt="The agent proposes a task in a channel and waits for approval" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
  <figcaption>The agent proposes a task in a channel and waits for approval</figcaption>
</figure>

It runs on your device, with your own model. Other people's messages in the
channel reach it as background, never as instructions, so nobody else can steer
your agent by writing to it.

</figure>

## Apps work together

Open a note's menu and choose Create task. Tasks adds a task named after the
note to your inbox. This is the first of what I call app tools: one app offering
an action that another app can use.

<figure>
  <img src="/screenshots/0.16.0/notes-create-task-menu.webp" alt="Create task in a note's menu, provided by Tasks" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
  <figcaption>Create task in a note's menu, provided by Tasks</figcaption>
</figure>

The first time, Brainstorm asks. The question shows both apps by name and by
their real identifier, so an app cannot pass itself off as another one.

<figure>
  <img src="/screenshots/0.16.0/shell-tool-approval.webp" alt="Brainstorm asks before one app runs another app's tool" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

</figure>

## Publish a website

Choose Publish website on a note. The page and its sub-pages become ordinary web
pages in a ZIP, styled with your current theme. Host it anywhere that serves
files.

<figure>
  <img src="/screenshots/0.16.0/notes-publish-website.webp" alt="Publishing a note as a website" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
  <figcaption>Publishing a note as a website</figcaption>
</figure>

Before anything is written, Brainstorm lists every page that will be included.
Pages that are only linked are offered but left unticked. Nothing outside the
list is published, and links to anything else are removed from the output.

## More embeds

Paste a link from Google Maps, OpenStreetMap, Spotify, SoundCloud, CodePen or
Miro. They join YouTube, Vimeo, Loom, Figma and CodeSandbox.

An embed shows a card first. It connects to the provider only when you press
Load.

## Safer sharing

Who can change the member list of something you share is now enforced. Only
owners can add people, remove people or change roles, and the person who created
a shared object always stays its owner. Until now an editor could do all three.

Removing someone from a channel is complete. They can no longer edit earlier
messages, their own included, and the messages are re-keyed for the people who
remain.

Chat between people also behaves as you would expect. A message from someone
else appears in an open channel straight away, an edit to a message reaches
everyone, and "You" names only your own messages.

If you share with someone still on 0.15, sharing keeps working. One change: only
the original owner of an object can invite someone who is on 0.16.

## Sync on your own network

Finding your other devices on a local network no longer reveals your computer's
name or its other network addresses, and a device stays discoverable after the
first ten minutes.

One thing to know: a device on 0.15 and one on 0.16 will not find each other
automatically on a local network. Pairing by address and syncing through a relay
work as before. Update both and discovery is back.

## Your first day

A new vault opens on your dashboard. It used to open on the full release
history. The Create vault button is always in view, and the starter content is
right: the first journal entry no longer says "0 words" next to its own text.

## Fixes and polish

**Chat.** Day dividers use your local time. A message sent just after midnight
used to sit under the previous day.

**Books.** The library, the properties panel and the footer agree on how far you
have read, and the panel no longer covers the next-page button.

<figure>
  <img src="/screenshots/0.16.0/books-progress.webp" alt="Books showing the same progress everywhere" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
  <figcaption>Books showing the same progress everywhere</figcaption>
</figure>

**Database.** Number columns line up from the header to the total, and a value
with no colour is a visible chip in light themes.

<figure>
  <img src="/screenshots/0.16.0/database-grid.webp" alt="A Database grid with aligned numbers and visible chips" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
  <figcaption>A Database grid with aligned numbers and visible chips</figcaption>
</figure>

**Books again.** Books that would not open now do, including FB2 files and EPUBs
with a damaged table of contents. If one still cannot be opened, Books says why.

**Files.** Emails no longer fill your vault with rows called "untitled". They are
named by their subject and stay in Mailbox.

**Agent.** It no longer answers with raw data or lists its own tools. If it has
no answer, it says so.

**Also.** The Browser shows the start page on a restored blank tab. The icon
picker shows emoji again in the installed app. Filter, Sort and View in Files
share one look. The Tasks checkbox is easy to see in dark themes.

Existing installs update from Settings, Updates.
