People have started using Brainstorm, so I did what a new person does: installed
it, made a vault, and opened every app once. Then I went through all twenty apps
again on a full vault, in light and in dark, reading each screen. This release
is what that turned up.

## Your first day

**A new vault opens on your dashboard.** It used to open on "What's new", paged
through every release since the first one. That says nothing to someone who
arrived a minute ago. It still appears after an update.

**Create vault is always in view.** On the step where you choose a starting
point, the card was taller than the window and the button sat below the bottom
edge.

  <figcaption>Choosing a starting point, with the buttons in view</figcaption>
</figure>

**The starter content is right.** The first journal entry no longer says "0
words" and "No entry yet" next to its own text, and the help bookmark opens the
documentation. Chat tells you to start your first channel when there are none.

## Fixes

**Icon picker.** Emoji are emoji again in the installed app. The Upload tab is a
drop zone, and it and Library are there in every app.

**Chat.** Day dividers use your local time. They used the date in UTC while the
times next to them were local, so anyone outside UTC saw messages under the
wrong day.

**Books.** The library, the properties panel and the reader footer showed three
different numbers for how far you had read. They now show one. The panel sits
beside the reader and leaves the next-page button alone.

<figure>
  <img src="/screenshots/0.15.1/books-progress.webp" alt="Books showing the same progress in the library, the panel and the footer" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
  <figcaption>Books showing the same progress in the library, the panel and the footer</figcaption>
</figure>

**Browser.** A blank tab from your last session shows the start page. It used to
show an empty grey window.

<figure>
  <img src="/screenshots/0.15.1/browser-start-page.webp" alt="The Browser start page on a restored blank tab" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
  <figcaption>The Browser start page on a restored blank tab</figcaption>

**Split view.** Each pane leaves room for the window buttons on its own edge
only.

## Polish

**Database.** Number columns line up from the header to the total. A value with
no colour is a visible chip in light themes. Lists read "Code files", not
"CodeFiles".

<figure>
  <img src="/screenshots/0.15.1/database-grid.webp" alt="A Database grid with aligned numbers and visible stage chips" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
  <figcaption>A Database grid with aligned numbers and visible stage chips</figcaption>
</figure>

**Files.** Filter, Sort and View share one look. The sort menu shows its last
row, and the selection bar stays clear of the files under it.

**Tasks.** The checkbox ring is easy to see in dark themes.

<figure>
  <img src="/screenshots/0.15.1/tasks-dark.webp" alt="Tasks in a dark theme" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
  <figcaption>Tasks in a dark theme</figcaption>
</figure>

**Every app.** The menu button in the header looks as available as it is. The
sidebars in Chat and Preview match the rest. Several messages say "objects"
where they said "entities".

Existing installs update from Settings, Updates.
