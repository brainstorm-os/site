Work with the agent. The AI isn't just a chat box — ask it in your own words and
it **drafts real objects** into your vault: a contact, a follow-up task, a
calendar event, a note. You review each draft, tweak anything it got wrong, then
**approve what you want to keep**. Nothing is saved until you say so — the agent
proposes, you commit, and it can't write to your vault on its own.

Your work syncs across devices on the same network — two machines share a vault
**directly, with no relay server** in between. The whole app now speaks four more
languages — **French, German, Italian, and Portuguese** — and you can bring your
notes in from **Notion**, blocks and all, as clean Markdown.

Plus a batch of editor fixes: uploaded **video and audio render inline** again,
forms support **conditional fields**, and apps can show a **notification badge**
on their dashboard icon.
